Insert variable:
Equation format: use V1, I1, V2, I2; internal names like Vx are also allowed. Examples: V1 - 2*I1 - I2 = 0, Vx/2 + (Vx-V2)/3 - I1 = 0. Replace resistor symbols with their numerical values first; use explicit multiplication such as 2*I1. Linear expressions, parentheses and multiplication/division by numbers are supported.

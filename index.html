Equation Editor

Enter circuit equations; internal variables are supported.

STEP 1
Variables:Operators:
Equation format: Type V1, I1, V2, I2 and they automatically display as V₁, I₁, V₂, I₂; each port variable is parsed as one variable. Internal names like Vx are also allowed. Examples: V₁ − 2I₁ − I₂ = 0, Vx/2 + (Vx−V₂)/3 − I₁ = 0. Replace resistor symbols with their numerical values first; use explicit multiplication such as 2*I1. Linear expressions, parentheses and multiplication/division by numbers are supported. Enter your KCL/KVL equations; the app calculates the parameter ratios for you.

Results

Two-port parameters calculated automatically.

STEP 2

ABCD convention: I2 enters port 2, so V1 = A·V2 − B·I2 and I1 = C·V2 − D·I2. A matrix marked unavailable requires a singular conversion or more independent circuit equations. The solver handles linear equations only.
